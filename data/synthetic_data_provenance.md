# Synthetic case provenance and reproducibility boundary

The 60 English script cases are **project-authored fictional examples**, not public ads or CHANEL submissions. The exact evaluated rows are available in [`primary_cases.jsonl`](primary_cases.jsonl) and [`extension_cases_v2.jsonl`](extension_cases_v2.jsonl), with their frozen [primary](frozen_manifest.json) and [extension](extension_frozen_manifest_v2.json) checksums. [`combined_cases_v3_60.jsonl`](combined_cases_v3_60.jsonl) is the ordered concatenation with a `dataset_split` field on each row; its own [manifest](combined_manifest_v3_60.json) records its checksum. `python scripts/verify_synthetic_dataset.py` verifies all three byte-level hashes, 10/10/10 verdict balance in each split, unique case IDs, and that the combined rows exactly match the two sources apart from the split marker.

**What is reproducible:** the precise saved inputs, labels, split membership, keyword evaluation, and scoring against saved AI outputs. The cases and rulebook are committed as inspectable data; the verifier will fail if their bytes or labels change. The first 30 were used for development and prompt revision. The second 30 were authored within the same project and later used to check the revised prompt; they are a supplementary synthetic development check, not an independent real-world test. The reviewer-return and adjudication files linked from [`README.md`](README.md) preserve label disputes and later proposals without silently changing scored labels. The extension freeze manifest's `UNTESTED_HOLDOUT` field describes its state **at freeze time**, before the saved later API run, and should not be read as its present evaluation status.

**What is not reproducible:** the original act of writing these exact sentences. No original generation prompt, template parameters, random seed, or script was saved when the cases were authored. A generator invented now would not prove how the historical cases were made. The set was deliberately balanced and constructed around disclosure, placement, competitor-impartiality, first-hand experience, and factual-claim boundaries; it is not a natural sample of actual creator work. The project-authored labels were revisited with peer feedback, but reviewer identity and experience were not established, and targeted later adjudication found disputed boundaries. These limits are material when interpreting the saved 30-case scores.

For a **new** synthetic set, the repeatable protocol would be: freeze the rulebook version; write a scenario matrix with target verdict, applicable rule IDs, positive and negative controls, caption/speech/on-screen fields, creator-use status and claim-source status **before** composing each script; record any generator prompt, template and seed; draft cases without showing model outputs; obtain independent rule-level labels and resolve disagreements; then freeze the JSONL and manifest before evaluation. This is a prospective protocol, not a claim about how the current 60 historical rows were produced.
