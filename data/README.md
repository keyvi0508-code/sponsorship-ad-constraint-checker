# Evaluation dataset

## Real public-content feasibility pilot

[`real_public_reels_pilot_v1.jsonl`](real_public_reels_pilot_v1.jsonl) records ten original public Instagram Reel URLs, observed creator/date/caption signals, and missing-evidence fields checked on 2026-10-01. These are real posts, not project-authored scripts. The [pilot report](../reports/real_public_reels_pilot_v1.md) explains the convenience-sample selection, source checks, and why **no full policy verdict or model accuracy** is reported. `python scripts/summarize_real_pilot.py` validates the inventory and reproduces its counts. The file contains short factual summaries and disclosure markers, not copied full captions or media. Posts can change after the access date.

The separate [public-caption intake protocol](../docs/public_content_intake_protocol.md) explains why these marker notes cannot be fed into the full-script reviewer as if unobserved video scenes were absent. `python scripts/inspect_public_caption_intake.py` checks readiness without a model call. All ten current rows need original caption capture before semantic review; its `UNKNOWN` statuses describe missing input, not model predictions or new gold labels.

The later [23-post caption development file](real_public_reels_caption_development_v1.jsonl) supplies original-page visible caption text for the first ten plus thirteen additional 2026 posts selected through gift, brand-mention, and experience queries. Its [manifest](real_public_reels_caption_development_v1_manifest.json) records order and checksum; the [selection record](../reports/public_caption_source_selection_v1.md) explains inclusion and bias. All 23 pass the caption-intake readiness check, while all 23 retain full-review `UNKNOWN` because only captions were captured. These are not independently labelled compliance cases. Run `python scripts/inspect_public_caption_intake.py data/real_public_reels_caption_development_v1.jsonl` to reproduce readiness counts without API use; `python scripts/evaluate_public_captions.py` reproduces the free caption-only keyword results. The [AI pilot report](../reports/public_caption_ai_pilot_v1.md) compares the saved two-stage AI and keyword suggestions without claiming full-video accuracy.

## Provenance and structure

The 60 rows are project-authored fictional drafts designed to exercise wording, placement, product-use knowledge, and ambiguous comparisons under a narrow interpretation of one public guide. They are **not** scraped advertisements or actual CHANEL submissions. There is no committed original generator prompt/script that can regenerate these exact rows; the committed case files and checksums preserve what was evaluated, while that missing generation provenance limits reproducibility. The [synthetic provenance note](synthetic_data_provenance.md) and `python scripts/verify_synthetic_dataset.py` distinguish verification of saved evaluation inputs from reproduction of their original authoring process. The extension was composed and reviewed separately from the first 30 cases, but both splits originate in this project and are not a representative sample of creator content.

Each JSONL row has a case ID, language/format, caption lines, scene-level spoken and on-screen lines, endorsement indicators, whether multiple brands appear, creator product-use status (`true`, `false`, or `null` for unknown), claim reference sources, and a `ground_truth` object with verdict, rule IDs, and rationale. The three permitted verdicts are `PASS` (no apparent in-scope issue), `FLAG` (clear breach), and `HUMAN_REVIEW` (material unresolved ambiguity). These are proposed *script* labels, not verification of a finished video. The benchmark runner removes `ground_truth` and `annotator_notes` before model calls; see the [evaluation guide](../reports/README.md) for the exact scoring and saved outputs.

`primary_cases.jsonl` contains 30 fictional English short-video script instances written to evaluate this prototype: 10 `PASS`, 10 `FLAG`, and 10 `HUMAN_REVIEW`. They are not real CHANEL creator scripts, endorsements, or private campaign materials. The v2 labels are frozen; their SHA-256 checksum and date are recorded in [frozen_manifest.json](frozen_manifest.json).

The first independent-label packet and later v2 packet are retained for audit history. The v2 return and adjudication are summarized in [the report](../reports/peer_review_return_v2_adjudication.md). Targeted reviews identified possible label-boundary issues and reviewer disagreement on three competitor-opinion cases; see the [first targeted adjudication record](../reports/targeted_adjudication_return_v1.md) and [latest targeted return](../reports/targeted_primary_adjudication_return_v2.md). A [v3 annotation decision guide draft](annotation_decision_guide_v3_draft.md) clarifies the disputed operational boundaries, but does not silently change the frozen labels or reported scores. Reviewer identity/provenance was not supplied, so the returns must not be presented as verified independent inter-annotator reliability.

The [boundary-case audit](../reports/boundary_case_audit_v1.md) identifies similar cases across both splits, including possible cross-rule omissions. The [blinded re-review packet](boundary_case_blind_review_packet_v1.md) hides the existing case IDs and labels and asks the reviewer to assess every applicable rule. The packet was returned; see the preserved [raw response](../reports/blind_peer_review_return_v1_raw.txt), the [initial review analysis](../reports/blind_peer_review_adjudication_v1.md), and the completed [rule-by-rule adjudication](../reports/boundary_case_adjudication_v1.md). The initial 11-case raw agreement was 7/11 (63.6%) against current labels; this targeted sample is not a reliability estimate. The [sensitivity analysis](../reports/label_adjudication_sensitivity_v1.md) explains why the later label changes must not be turned into a new headline score. Original labels and reported scores remain unchanged.

The cases test disclosure wording and sponsor identity, disclosure placement across structured caption/speech/on-screen fields, competitor criticism, first-hand experience, and unsupported factual claims. The prototype removes `ground_truth` and `annotator_notes` before sending a case to either system. Results on this small, purpose-built set describe only these cases and do not establish real-world brand-review performance.

The rule IDs used in the review packet are project identifiers mapped to the public CHANEL guide; they are not official CHANEL identifiers. Keep the private blind-ID mapping under `work/` local and do not publish it with the review packet.

## Supplementary 30-case extension

`extension_cases_v1.jsonl` adds 10 cases per verdict class as supplementary coverage beyond the primary set. The randomized [blind packet](extension_blind_review_packet_v1.md) and [blank response template](extension_review_response_template_v1.md) were returned for all 30 cases. Initial agreement was 21/30 (70.0%). The [adjudication report](../reports/extension_peer_review_adjudication_v1.md) records two label changes approved by the project owner; the resulting balanced v2 set is frozen in [extension_cases_v2.jsonl](extension_cases_v2.jsonl), with checksum in [extension_frozen_manifest_v2.json](extension_frozen_manifest_v2.json). The extension reviewer did not provide identity or relevant experience, so 70% is raw agreement from one reviewer return, not verified inter-rater reliability.

The combined, versioned 60-case file is [combined_cases_v3_60.jsonl](combined_cases_v3_60.jsonl). Keep its two 30-case splits separate in reports: the original set is the primary development evaluation; the extension is a supplementary prompt-locked development check. The extension results are keyword baseline 20/30 (66.7%) and AI 23/30 (76.7%); see the [evaluation report](../reports/holdout_keyword_baseline_results.md). Do not headline one combined score or present the extension as real-world evidence. The original extension, packet, and draft manifest remain for auditability. The private ID mapping is ignored by Git.
