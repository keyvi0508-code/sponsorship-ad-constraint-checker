# Blind peer-review instructions

## Purpose and source access

Independently label these 30 fictional English short-video scripts before comparing your decisions with the project author's draft labels. The CHANEL page may render without its body text in some browsers. The self-contained, project-written rule summaries below are paraphrases of the numbered sections on CHANEL's public [Social Media Guidelines](https://www.chanel.com/us/makeup/social-media-guidelines/) (accessed 2026-09-29). The page is the source of the rules, but it may not load reliably for every reviewer.

These scripts are synthetic academic examples, not actual CHANEL campaigns or creator posts. This project is not affiliated with CHANEL and is not legal advice.

## Project rulebook and source mapping

The IDs below were created for this project; they are **not CHANEL's official rule IDs**. Each maps to the corresponding numbered section of the linked guide.

### CH-DISC-01 — Disclose the material connection and identify CHANEL

**Source mapping:** CHANEL guide sections 1 (“Disclose Connection to Chanel”) and 2, “Wording.”

When a creator posts about or endorses CHANEL or its products, disclose the material connection clearly and conspicuously. A material connection includes payment, free products or services, gifts, travel, special access, or another benefit. The guide gives examples such as `#sponsored`, `#paid`, `#ad`, `#ChanelPartner`, and `#GiftFromChanel`. If multiple brands appear, a generic label may not make clear that CHANEL is the sponsor, so the sponsor should be identified. The guide says a bare thank-you, `#partner`, `#ambassador`, abbreviated labels such as `#spon`, `#sp`, `#collab`, and labels such as `#gifted`, `#freeproduct`, `#comped`, or `#hosted` are insufficient on their own.

### CH-DISC-02 — Put disclosure near the endorsement and in the matching medium

**Source mapping:** CHANEL guide section 2, “Placement” and “A few other tips.”

Place the disclosure near the endorsement and at the beginning of the post. If it is in a caption, the guide says to put it in the first two lines, before a “more” link. Disclosure should match the medium: spoken when the endorsement is spoken, and on-screen when the endorsement is shown visually. A disclosure only in the video description does not replace disclosure within the video. This review packet contains proposed script text only; it cannot establish actual font size, visibility, on-screen duration, audibility, or repetition in a finished video.

### CH-CONTEXT-01 — Do not imply impartiality when criticizing a competitor

**Source mapping:** CHANEL guide section 1.

A creator with a CHANEL relationship should not represent or imply that criticism of a CHANEL competitor is impartial. A neutral competitor mention, without criticism or an impartiality implication, is not by itself a breach of this specific rule. Use `HUMAN_REVIEW` if the wording or surrounding context makes the implication genuinely unclear.

### CH-CLAIM-01 — Opinions need first-hand use; factual claims need support

**Source mapping:** CHANEL guide sections 3 (“Give Your Honest and Truthful Opinions”) and 4 (“Only Make Factual Statements That Are Truthful and Can Be Verified”).

An endorsement, review, or opinion about a product should reflect the creator's honest experience, and the creator should have used the product or feature. Factual statements about CHANEL or its products should be true, supportable, and verifiable. The packet does not include external substantiation, so do not decide an objective claim is true or false based only on its wording. Use `HUMAN_REVIEW` when first-hand use or factual support cannot be established from the supplied information; use `FLAG` only when the provided metadata clearly contradicts a first-hand claim.

## Verdicts

- `PASS`: no in-scope issue is apparent from the supplied information.
- `FLAG`: the supplied script or metadata clearly conflicts with a rule above.
- `HUMAN_REVIEW`: context, actual video execution, first-hand use, or claim substantiation cannot be resolved from the supplied information.

## Review task

For each case in the packet, fill in the verdict, relevant project rule ID(s), and a brief rationale naming the evidence. Review only the supplied script and metadata; do not assume facts about an actual finished video. Use an empty rule-ID list for `PASS`. Make your decision independently; do not ask to see or infer the draft label. Do not reorder the case inputs. Return the completed packet separately and keep the original unchanged.
