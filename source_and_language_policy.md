# Rule-source and bilingual evaluation protocol

This project will use only publicly accessible, authoritative rules from the selected brand and will record the URL, access date, and exact relevant passages. Each implemented rule will include a source excerpt, a concise operational interpretation, and any ambiguity or limitations.

English and Chinese test cases must express the same underlying scenario so language effects can be compared. A Chinese translation or paraphrase of an English official source is a project translation, not a claim that the brand issued a Chinese-language rule. Preserve the source language alongside any translation.

Do not infer private campaign requirements (such as undisclosed product selling points or competitor blacklists). If a rule is not publicly supported, leave it out or clearly label it as a separate hypothetical stress test rather than an official brand rule.

The prototype is a research/coursework aid and not legal advice or a substitute for the brand's current approval workflow. Re-check source pages before final submission in case they change.
