# Language and evidence policy

The primary dataset and prototype use English only, matching the language of CHANEL's official Social Media Guidelines. This avoids adding translation quality as a confounding variable in a small 30-case evaluation.

Use the official source URL and record the access date. Clearly distinguish source-grounded rule text from project operational interpretations. Do not invent private campaign requirements, product selling points, or a competitor blacklist and attribute them to CHANEL.

The test scripts are constructed research examples, not actual campaign briefs or confidential creator submissions. The prototype supports human review; it is not legal advice, does not approve or publish content, and does not claim CHANEL affiliation or approval.
